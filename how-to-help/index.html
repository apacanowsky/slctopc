Who Can Actually Move This Forward

The immediate goal here isn't asking anyone to "build SLC to PC." It's asking what needs to happen for the already-mapped Parleys Vision Corridor and the Mountain Dell–Parleys Summit Gap Closure (R2-046) to advance from a line on a map into a funded feasibility and alignment study. That's a much smaller, much more answerable question — and these are the people and bodies positioned to answer it.

Cyclists Vote.

Every name below is either elected or answers to someone who is. Showing up, in numbers, with a specific and reasonable ask, is how a mapped project becomes a funded one.

Stephanie Tomlin

Director, UDOT Trails Division

Her team manages the Utah Trail Network and its master plan — she's closest to the actual project list and how it gets prioritized.

Ask: what it would take to move R2-046 (Parley's Trail Gap) and the Parleys Vision Corridor segment below it into a funded feasibility and alignment study.

Geoff Dupaix

UDOT Region 2 Planning Manager (Salt Lake & Summit Counties)

Region 2 covers both counties this trail would cross, and helps set regional planning priorities within UDOT.

Ask: the same question, framed regionally — this segment matters to both counties Region 2 serves.

gdupaix@utah.gov

Natalie Gochnour

Utah Transportation Commissioner, Region 2 (Salt Lake, Summit & Tooele Counties)

The Transportation Commission is what ultimately approves Utah Trail Network funding decisions.

Ask: her support for prioritizing R2-046 when the Commission considers future Trail Network funding rounds.

Wasatch Front Regional Council (WFRC)

Regional Transportation Planning

WFRC's active-transportation staff and long-range regional plans shape which projects are eligible for funding on the Salt Lake side.

Ask: whether this segment is, or could be, reflected in WFRC's regional active transportation plan.

Salt Lake County, Emigration Canyon & Summit County

Local Elected Officials & Planners

UDOT specifically points to local government support, and inclusion in adopted local transportation plans, as key to advancing state trail projects.

Ask: your county council member or planning staff whether this connection is reflected in local transportation or trails plans — and to formally support it if not.
